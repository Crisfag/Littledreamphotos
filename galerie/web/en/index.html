Good questions, straight answers.

Is Holypixx available in English?

Yes. Your clients' side is fully in English: galleries, emails, print shop, payment pages and invoices. Your dashboard is in English too for everything you need to run galleries: creating them, styles, proofing, HD delivery, print shop, billing and settings. Online booking, contracts and client records are still in French while we translate them: join the list and we'll email you once, when they open in English.

Can a client download the photos?

Not while they choose: photos are cut into tiles and reassembled on screen, so there is no file to download. Once the session is done, you upload the final files and they download them in high resolution, as a ZIP or one by one.

Can you stop a screenshot?

No website in the world can. Holypixx makes a screenshot useless (low resolution, dense watermark), emails you when one is attempted, and finds the source gallery of an image from its invisible fingerprint.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They get a link and a password, nothing else.

Can I customize how my galleries look?

Yes, on every plan. Nine styles (classic, soft, architectural, modern, night, editorial, romantic, boho, art gallery), each with its palette, shapes and fonts. You can change the heading font (13 to choose from) and the text font (10), write a welcome note, pick the cover photo or put a YouTube or Vimeo film at the top of the gallery, ideal for a wedding.

Where do you ship prints?

Across Europe, and to the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, South Korea and the United Arab Emirates once you set an international shipping rate. Our partner lab Prodigi prints locally or as close as possible, and ships with tracking.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. On each online payment (extras, prints), only payment fees are deducted: 2 % + €0.30, what the card and the transfer to your account cost.

Can I have a web address in my name?

Yes, with the Pro plan: your galleries and portfolio open on your-studio.holypixx.com, with your studio's name.

Where are the photos hosted?

On Cloudflare's infrastructure (R2 storage and D1 database). Passwords are never stored in plain text and every account is strictly isolated.
